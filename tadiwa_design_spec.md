# Portfolio Design Spec — Tadiwanashe Guchu

> **Source:** [portfolio by Elivi Guiho on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/15011636-portfolio)
> **Type:** Personal Portfolio — Hero / Landing Page
> **Subject:** Software Developer

---

## Overview

A personal portfolio landing page hero section for a software developer named **Tadiwanashe Guchu**. The design uses a bold typographic layout with a single dominant accent color (orange) against a warm blush background, creating a modern, energetic, and strongly branded identity.

## Typography

### Hero Headline — "TADIWANASHE GUCHU"

- **Style:** All-caps, ultra-bold, condensed display
- **Suggested fonts:** Anton, Black Han Sans, or Bebas Neue
- **Weight:** Black / 900
- **Size:** Very large (display scale, ~96–120px equivalent)
- **Color:** Near-black (`#1A1A2E`), with "GUCHU" in orange (`#F05A28`)

### Body Copy

- **Text:** _"I'm a software developer based in Harare, Zimbabwe."_
- **Style:** Small, light-weight sans-serif
- **Weight:** Regular / 400
- **Color:** Near-black

### Navigation Links

- **Items:** My Work · Formations · About me
- **Style:** Small, medium-weight sans-serif
- **Weight:** Medium / 500

### CTA Buttons — "HIRE ME" / "Contact"

- **Style:** Uppercase or small-caps sans-serif, bold
- **Weight:** Bold / 700

---

## Layout

### Grid / Composition

- **Structure:** Full-width hero section with a layered typographic + photo composition
- **Left side:** Large headline "TADIWANASHE" + subtitle + CTA button
- **Center:** Cut-out photo of the developer (no background), with an orange radial glow behind
- **Right side / overlap:** "GUCHU" in large orange type, overlapping the photo

### Navigation Bar

- **Left:** Orange logo mark (dot cluster icon)
- **Center:** Text nav links (My Work, Formations, About me)
- **Right:** Filled orange pill button ("Contact")
- **Style:** Minimal, no border/shadow

### Social Icons

- **Icons:** Instagram, Twitter, Facebook
- **Position:** Bottom-right of the hero section
- **Style:** Dark/black monochrome icons

---

## Design Notes

- The orange prop (bag) held by the subject intentionally echoes the brand accent color, unifying the photo with the UI palette.
- The split headline ("TADIWANASHE" left / "GUCHU" right) with the photo in the center creates a dynamic typographic frame around the subject.
- The design works as a single-screen hero — no scrolling needed to communicate the core message and CTA.
- Social proof / credibility is subtly communicated via social media links in the footer of the hero.
